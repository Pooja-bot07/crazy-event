01 Baby Shower
Consult

Walk the space

We visit the venue, take real measurements, and talk through your date, guest count, and must-haves.

Design

Structural plan

A drawn layout of arches, drape lines, and tables, priced line by line before anything is ordered.

Build

Install day

Our crew builds the frame first, then dresses it — florals, linen, lighting — on a fixed install window.

Strike

Same-night teardown

We clear the space on your venue's schedule, so nothing is left for you or your venue to manage.
